What this is

Grimdark Future: Warfleets is a solo game of fleet battles against an AI opponent, played on a 72″ × 48″ table. It's an unofficial fan project and isn't affiliated with One Page Rules.

Playing a game

  • Setup: press New game (top right) and choose a 300 or 450 point game, then objective markers are placed first (you and the AI take turns), then both fleets deploy. Both fleets cost exactly the game size, and Heroes and Titles are assigned to ships at random.
  • Deploying: pick a unit from the list under the board (it scrolls sideways if needed), then hold and drag (or click) inside the blue zone to place it. After placing, rotate it (hold and drag, or move the mouse), then confirm. The ghost is orange over difficult or dangerous terrain and red where it cannot go. A Vanguard then shows a line in front of it: drag or click along the line to choose how far it advances.
  • Taking damage: when one of your ships is hit, a window asks which upgrade takes the damage (it then keeps taking damage until it is disabled). If you have a Tactical Master, you can deploy it at the start of any round after the first, or press Keep in reserve to wait.
  • Rounds: a game lasts 4 rounds. Each round has four phases in order: Squadrons, Light ships, Medium ships, Heavy ships. In a phase, you and the AI alternate activating one unit of that class at a time.
  • Activating: click one of your units with a white ring, use any optional abilities (each has its own button in the middle of the bottom bar), then choose Hold, Move + shoot, Cruise or Ram (ships only) from the buttons along the bottom. Buttons that don’t apply right now are greyed out. Abilities with a reach (mines, Avenger, carrier launch, Repair Bay, Tractor Beam) show a ring under the ship; a click outside it places the effect at the closest point inside. Ships move straight and pivot once, at any point from halfway to the end of the move: aim where you want the ship to end up (move the mouse and left-click; on a touch screen hold and drag anywhere, then press Confirm). The faint outline shows everywhere the move can end, and the white dot is where it will pivot. Right-click (mouse) or long-press (touch) steps back or cancels whatever you are doing.
  • Shooting: after moving, click an enemy to open its weapon list, then press Fire for each weapon that can reach it. A red ring marks enemies in reach, and hovering one lists the weapons that can fire at it. Dashed rings under your ship show each weapon’s range. Your activation ends by itself once no weapon has a target left.
  • Game rules (Game rules tab): Core rules by default. Advanced rules adds the Beginner’s Guide terrain pieces (planets, asteroid fields, nebulae, stations, stars and so on) from the next game. Sensors is an optional crutch for you: AI units are only visible while one of your units has them inside its turret range, or after they fire on you (they then stay visible for the rest of the game). The Black Box (bottom right) only lists what you can see.
  • Objectives: markers can be picked up and carried; the score line at the top shows markers carried by each side.
  • Terrain: cover, difficult, impassable and dangerous terrain are all in play. Hover a terrain piece for its rule names, or click it to see the full rules on the card; a unit standing inside terrain is noted under its card.

Inspecting units

Click any unit to show its card (Sensor Readings, top right): name, type, special rules, systems and weapons with their facing, and the Move, Cruise, Eva and Tou boxes. Damage boxes fill in as a ship or squadron is hurt. Hover (or tap) a rule, system, Hero or Title on the card to read what it does, and distances are measured from base edge to base edge. Bases and centre dots are only drawn when something would connect with them (a target in reach, a ram, a collision).

Settings

  • Colours (Icons & colours tab): pick the fleet colour for you and for the AI. Your choice is remembered on this device.
  • Icons (Icons & colours tab): each ship and squadron type has a PNG icon. Upload your own to replace one, or use Reset to go back to the built-in design. Square PNGs with a transparent background, pointing up, work best; they are tinted with the fleet colour unless the picture has a solid background. Uploaded icons are stored on this device only.
  • Display: unit image size (the pictures only; bases, click targets and ranges stay the same) and an option to open in full screen.

Rule set

Off = Core rules only: the four basic terrain types (cover, difficult, impassable, dangerous) as plain zones. On adds the Beginner’s Guide terrain: named pieces (Planet, Asteroid Field, Nebula, Particle Nebula, Mine Field, Space Station, Heavy and Light Debris, Star) with blocking sight, sight screens, Heavy Debris cruise damage and the Star danger zone. Takes effect when the next game starts.

Optional rules

You can only see an AI unit while one of your ships or squadrons has it within its turret range, or after it fires on you (it then stays visible for the rest of the game). Out of range it disappears again. This only limits what you see; the AI is unchanged. Sensor Readings only record what you can see.

Unit image size

Only changes how big the ship pictures are drawn. Bases, click targets, ranges and rules are unchanged.

Full screen

Press Esc to leave full screen. On tablets and phones it switches itself back on at the next tap if you swipe out of it (unless you used the exit button). iPhones don’t allow web pages to go full screen; add the app to the Home Screen instead. For the installed app to hide the system bars on its own, set "display": "fullscreen" in manifest.webmanifest and reinstall.
